About

Image processing is a team sport

I am a doctoral researcher at Tampere University working on image processing — restoration, sensor artefacts, and the gap between what a camera records and what an operator needs to decide.

The part I keep coming back to is the collaboration: sitting with the people who will use the output, arguing about what “good enough” means, and shipping something they keep using after the project ends.

Next I want to move further towards that side — leading teams, building products, and turning research into companies. If that is what you are doing, I would like to talk.
