About

Adding Reindeer to Carbonara (not yet)

I'm a 34-year-old who, for the better part of three decades, was a full-time Roman; navigating ancient cobblestones, missing delayed buses and surviving a chaotic life with a certain flair was my go-to. About 10 years ago, I decided to swap the Colosseum for the cold, and I've been successfully faking it as a Finn ever since. My life is a delightful mess of cultural confusion, where I'm constantly trying to remember if I'm supposed to be sipping espresso or enduring a sauna session. The Roman in me still dreams of summer heat or chaotic football games at the stadium, but the new, faux-Finnish me has learned to appreciate the subtle art of not complaining about the weather. So, if you're looking for someone who brings a little bit of ancient history and a lot of dry humor to the table, you've got the right guy.
